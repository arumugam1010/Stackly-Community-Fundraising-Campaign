Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Donation Questions

How do I receive my tax deduction certificate?

Immediately upon transaction completion, a digital 80G / 501(c)(3) tax receipt and certificate is sent to your registered email address. You can also download all historical receipts from your personal donor dashboard anytime.

Can I donate anonymously without displaying my name?

Yes. When initiating your payment, simply tick the "Make this donation anonymous" checkbox. Your name will be hidden from the public campaign donor roll while your official receipt remains private and securely emailed to you.

How can I set up or modify a recurring monthly gift?

Select "Make this a monthly donation" on any cause card. You can pause, adjust the amount, or cancel your recurring contribution with a single click from your donor dashboard at any time without fees.

What payment methods are supported?

We support all major Visa, Mastercard, American Express cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, UPI, and direct bank wire transfers for institutional or large philanthropists.
