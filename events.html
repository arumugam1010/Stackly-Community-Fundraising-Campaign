Past Achievements

What We Achieved Together In Recent Drives

Every event brings tangible, life-saving change to underserved communities. Take a look at recent milestone achievements made possible by attendees and donors.

Winter Relief

Annual Winter Warmth Rally

Provided thermal blankets, woolens, and 30-day dry food hampers to 3,400 families across 8 remote villages.

Healthcare

Mobile Pediatric Health Camp

Screened 1,120 children for chronic malnutrition, vision impairments, and dispensed free medicines.

Clean Water

Solar Borewell Groundbreaking

Installed 4 deep-aquifer solar water purification stations, eliminating a 4km daily water-carrying journey.

Education

Youth Coding & Literacy Fair

450 high schoolers completed hands-on digital skills modules, with 60 laptops donated to local school labs.

Community Organizing

How To Host Or Sponsor A Fundraiser

Want to turn your birthday, sports marathon, or corporate social responsibility drive into life-saving funds? Follow our simple 4-step hosting roadmap.

01

Submit Event Proposal

Tell us your event concept (5K run, bake sale, charity gala, or school drive) and desired beneficiary cause.

02

Receive Host Toolkit

Get branded banners, donor receipt QR codes, event checklists, and social media promo materials.

03

Host With Support

Our regional volunteer coordinators assist you with on-site logistics, live fundraising meters, and guest check-ins.

04

Track Real Impact

Within 14 days, your donors receive photos and audit reports showing exactly how every dollar was put to work.
