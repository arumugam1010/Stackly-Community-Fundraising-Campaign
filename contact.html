Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Support & Partnership Questions

Have an urgent question about donations, volunteer applications, or visiting our field programs? Find quick answers below.

How quickly will someone respond to my inquiry?

Our community helpdesk operates Monday through Saturday. All standard web form inquiries receive a response within 12 to 24 business hours. Urgent disaster reports received via our crisis hotline are dispatched immediately.

How can our company or brand partner for CSR sponsorships?

We work with national and global corporate partners on matching gift programs, sponsored school construction, and employee volunteer days. Select "Corporate Partnership" in the message form above or email directly to partnerships@stackly.org.

Can donors visit the field relief sites in person?

Yes. We organize quarterly verified donor exposure trips to our school centers, pediatric health clinics, and clean water wells. Please reach out at least 30 days in advance so our field security team can arrange permits and lodging.

Where can I access your annual financial and audit reports?

Our complete audited financial statements, tax 990 filings, and third-party impact assessments are publicly downloadable from our governance portal or by requesting a copy through our contact form.

24/7 Rapid Emergency Response

Reporting A Critical Local Disaster Or Urgent Community Need?

Our rapid-deployment field squadrons are on standby 24 hours a day to provide emergency water, hot rations, and medical evacuation across our regional chapter networks.
