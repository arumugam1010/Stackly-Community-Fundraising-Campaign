Where We Work

Regional Humanitarian Operations & Priority Corridors

Focusing our resources where medical vulnerability, climate vulnerability, and economic hardship are highest.

Southern Coastal Delta Corridor

Covering Cuddalore, Nagapattinam, and Cauvery delta fishing hamlets. Dedicated to seasonal monsoon flood mitigation, cyclone shelter supplies, and brackish groundwater purification.

45,000+ Coastal Villagers Served

Western Ghats Tribal Settlements

Operating mobile hill clinics in the Nilgiris, Anaimalai, and Jawadhu forest valleys. Delivering pediatric nutrition, maternal health checkups, and solar lighting to isolated indigenous settlements.

32,000+ Tribal Beneficiaries

Central Agricultural Drylands

Serving Salem, Dharmapuri, and Namakkal agricultural districts. Constructing solar-powered community borewells, running child nutrition centers, and providing interest-free micro-grants to widowed farm workers.

65,000+ Rural Families Assisted
